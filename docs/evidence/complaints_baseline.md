## Dispute definition

The source has no dispute label. `Cargo no reconocido` (unrecognized charge) is the only named subcategory of category `Transactions`; every category has exactly one named subcategory plus NULLs (`t16_complaint_subcategories.sql`). Three levels:

| level | definition | disputes in snapshot | per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| strict | Cargo no reconocido, case type Complaint or Claim | 10,480 | 3,487 |
| **central** (used by the ROI) | every Cargo no reconocido | 12,297 | 4,092 |
| broad | every Transactions complaint (incl. NULL subcategory) | 13,580 | 4,518 |

## Key facts

- Central disputes per snapshot year range from 4,044 to 4,201, so the snapshot is annualised as its average rate.
- 70% of central disputes are still Open or In Process at the snapshot date; resolved ones took a median of 15.0 days. Median first response in the main channel (Call Center): 37 h.
- Disputes behave like every other complaint: average resolution days 15.4 vs 15.7, SLA breach 20.4% vs 20.1%. The source does not model dispute-specific handling, so handling effort comes from `config/roi_assumptions.yaml`.
- Complaints and call-center contacts look independent: the share of complaints with a contact from the same customer in the 7 days before differs from a placebo window 90 days earlier by at most 0.4 percentage points, even for complaints received by the call center. Contacts per dispute cannot be measured.
- `claimed_amount` cannot size the money at stake: 67% of central disputes have no currency, and the median amount is about the same in MXN, COP, ARS and USD although their values differ by orders of magnitude.
- Fraud does not repeat on a card: 21 of 4,316 fraud transactions follow an earlier fraud on the same card, 0 within 37 h. A faster card block has no measurable loss avoided in this data (offline `is_fraud`, ADR 0003).
